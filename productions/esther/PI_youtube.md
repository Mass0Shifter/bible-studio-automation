## Description
**The Book Where God Is Never Named (But He's Everywhere)**

The Book of Esther is often told as a royal love story: an orphan girl who wins the king's heart and becomes queen. But it is one of the most suspenseful rescue stories in all of Scripture — and the only book in the Bible where the name of God never appears, yet His hand is on every page.

In this Prophetic Implication of the Book of Esther, discover the hidden hand of God's providence ("for such a time as this"), the ancient war against the promised Seed, Esther as a picture of Christ our Mediator who opens the throne of grace, and the great reversal that turned a day of death into a feast of joy — pointing us straight to the cross.
